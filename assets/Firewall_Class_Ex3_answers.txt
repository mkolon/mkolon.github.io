Firewall Class Exercise 3 Toolkit 
Questions and Doc Requests

1. Firewall
- We have the topology via slides, but we would ask for it if we did not.
- What model is it (need to check EOL)?
- Who manages it? (we know Northwood via slides).   Request: Vendor contract, SLA.
- Do they submit reporting, frequency?  Request: reporting
- Is it audited? Frequency of audits?  Request: Audit (all audits are requested in prep week, but if we missed one we would ask)
- Change Management over the firewall?  Documentation, Reporting? Request: Reporting for change management
- SIEM? (We would ask essentially the same questions as above for the SIEM)
- How do these feed into the IR program?
- Check overall governance over these things and reporting up to the Board.

2. Segmentation
Model interview questions:
- How is guest network traffic kept separate from internal systems? 
- When was the last time the network diagram was updated and who updated it.
- What prevents a device on one network segment from reaching another?
- Has segmentation ever actually been tested? 
Model evidence to request:
- Network diagram showing segments or zones
- Review list of hardware and/or review server room to determine accuracy of network diagram.
- Results of any segmentation or penetration testing.
- Additionally, review the scope of the pen test review to ensure the scope of the test was adequate.
- VLAN or access control list configuration summary

3. Availability
Questions/Discussion Topics
- Who is responsible for restoration process / backups and testing?
- What is monitoring and notification process for outages in real time?
- When was the most recent backup / what is the backup frequency?
- BIA – How often is the network architecture reviewed to determine most important systems and single points of failure?
- (if not part of BIA) What reviews have been done to determine / test capacity to handle peak load? 
- What steps have been taken to provide failover options / redundancy?  What is the failover path? 
- When was the last live failure?
- What were the downtimes?  Within RTOs / compliant with SLAs
 
Request
- Contract with Northwood / SLAs
- Failover test results
- Backup Documentation
- RTO / RPO validation
- Capacity Management

4. Monitoring
- Who is responsible for monitoring logs?  Is it Tom, Northwoods, or a combination of both?  
- We would expect this to be defined within policy or in the contract with the MSP.
- What tool or platform is utilized for monitoring?
- Were any alerts generated turn out to be credible threats? (during a specified period of time)
 
Requests:
- List of alert reports that were generated (during a specified period of time)
- Any related policies
- Related reports that are presented to the Board or executive committee
